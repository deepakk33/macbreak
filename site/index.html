Install

Two ways in. Both take about a minute.

Homebrew
brew install --cask deepakk33/tap/macbreak
open -a MacBreak   # then tick "Start at login"
Or build from source
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/deepakk33/macbreak/main/scripts/bootstrap.sh | bash

Compiles on your machine — no Gatekeeper prompt. Needs xcode-select --install, not the full Xcode.

Questions

Does it work with multiple monitors?

Yes. Every display is covered, each with its own countdown and buttons.

Is MacBreak free?

Yes. Free and open source under the MIT licence. No paid tier, no account, no ads.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

Yes. Every build is universal — Apple Silicon and Intel — and runs on macOS 13 Ventura or later.

Is there a version of Safe Eyes for Mac?

Not officially. MacBreak covers the same ground natively. Read the comparison.

How do I follow the 20-20-20 rule?

Set "Look away every" to 20 min and "Look away for" to 20 sec in Preferences. More on the rule.

Does it phone home?

No. Zero network code. Nothing leaves your machine.

How do water reminders work?

Off by default. Enable in Preferences, and every 45 min (configurable) a 15-second overlay asks "drink or skip?" Drink resets the full timer; skip brings the next one sooner. All data stays local with weekly suggestions.

What if I'm in a meeting?

Snooze, or Pause from the menu bar. Pause stops all clocks until you resume.

How do I uninstall?

brew uninstall --cask macbreak or make uninstall from source. Both stop it and remove the login agent.

Why not Electron?

A break reminder at 200 MB is its own problem. This is AppKit — ~1,400 lines of Swift, ~10 MB of memory, zero dependencies.
